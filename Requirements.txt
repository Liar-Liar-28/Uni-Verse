Make sure vosk is listed in your requirements.txt:

vosk


Download an English model from Vosk Models
. For example:

wget https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models/vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15.zip
unzip vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15.zip -d ./models/


Place the model folder inside your project as ./models/vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15.
